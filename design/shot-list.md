# Shot list — what to photograph at the gym

The page's biggest remaining ceiling is real Matthews photography. Until these
exist, the design intentionally shows no placeholder or stock imagery.

## 1. Coach Nate action shot (unblocks the "Meet Coach Nate" image well)
- **Subject:** Nate mid-coaching — crouched next to a member at a rower or
  during a floor exercise, talking to them, both faces visible.
- **Framing:** landscape 3:2, subject in the left two-thirds, shot from
  slightly below eye level. Leave clean background (gym floor, FBBC signage).
- **Lighting:** session lighting as-is; avoid overhead fluorescent glare on
  faces. Golden-hour side light through the front glass works if available.
- **Replaces:** the removed placeholder well in `SalesLetter.tsx` (search
  "Image well intentionally removed"). Export ~1600×1067, JPG q80, save as
  `public/images/coach-nate.jpg` and restore the `<Image>` block from git
  history (`git show design-loop-v0:components/SalesLetter.tsx`).

## 2. Hero poster with a human face (raises first-viewport impact ceiling)
- **Subject:** Nate facing camera, mid-sentence, gesturing — a "he's about to
  tell me something" frame pulled from the VSL shoot or re-shot.
- **Framing:** 16:9, face in the right third (headline text breathes left),
  eyes toward lens.
- **Replaces:** `public/images/vsl-poster.jpg` (currently a branded template
  graphic with no person). Keep the baked-in title/duration text style.

## 3. Session wide shot (future section background / proof of community)
- **Subject:** a full class mid-workout, coach visible, genuine effort and
  smiles — 8–15 people so the room reads "community", not "crowd".
- **Framing:** wide, waist-height, minimal motion blur.
- **Use:** future dark-band background or a "the room you'll walk into"
  section; also ad creative.

## 4. Member celebration moment (retargeting + reviews section)
- **Subject:** high-five / fist-bump at the end of a session, or a member
  ringing a milestone bell if one exists.
- **Framing:** tight, candid, faces lit.
- **Use:** pairs with the Google-review quotes (Nikki, Allie, Eunice — get
  their photo consent if used next to their names).
